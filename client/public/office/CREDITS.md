# Office character sprites

`char_0.png` … `char_5.png` are 112×96 sheets: 3 rows (facing down, up, right;
left is the right row mirrored) × 7 frames of 16×32 px (3 walk, 2 typing,
2 reading).

- Characters: **MetroCity – Free Top Down Character Pack** by JIK-A-4
  (https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack), released
  under CC0 1.0.
- Sheets as prepared by **Pixel Agents** (https://github.com/pablodelucca/pixel-agents),
